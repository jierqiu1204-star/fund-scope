## Scope

This is a local code/schema baseline captured before implementation. It is not a VPS observation and MUST NOT count toward production rollout or validation evidence.

## Fail-closed contract baseline

The bounded contract batch passed `26` tests covering:

- rejection of raw/fallback forward prices and signal-close substitution;
- pending rather than fabricated future outcomes;
- exact separation of `production_published` and `research_replay` cohorts;
- rejection of current-survivor and future-known replay membership;
- prevention of production/replay evidence merging even when forward prices are shared.

## Schema and provenance baseline

- Local Alembic head: `20260715_000046`.
- `EtfSignalValidationRun` already has nullable `ranking_source_kind`, `source_signal_run_id`, and `source_replay_run_key` plus single-identity checks.
- There is no validation source-manifest hash or child source-event relation, so one run id cannot prove a multi-date aggregate.
- `EtfSyncCursor.scope` is the primary key and can support additive independent scope namespaces without a duplicate cursor table.

## Synchronization and scheduler baseline

- Same-day ETF sync defaults to batch size `20`, one batch, cursor scope `short_research_daily`.
- The post-close coverage path wraps one same-day slice in a `50` second async timeout.
- The historical backfill accepts `365/730/1095` days and calls `sync_short_research_data(..., sync_all_etfs=True)`.
- `sync_all_etfs=True` disables the batch-count bound and processes all prioritized batches serially.
- Selection freshness is based on the latest row date only; it does not independently prove 61-session or contract-derived depth.

## Resource baseline

- No production-shaped RSS/SQL benchmark exists yet; therefore no performance acceptance claim is made.
- Static bounds exist only for the same-day path (`20` codes by default and `50` seconds in the post-close caller).
- The historical path has no code, row, RSS, SQL, or durable intra-universe checkpoint bound and is the pre-change risk to remove.
