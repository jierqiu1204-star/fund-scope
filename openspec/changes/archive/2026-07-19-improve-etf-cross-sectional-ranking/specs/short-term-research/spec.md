## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: ETF final ranking uses versioned cross-sectional score
The system SHALL rank ETF short-term candidates with a versioned final score that combines cross-sectional percentile ranking, dynamic thresholds, data reliability, liquidity quality, and premium or discount risk. Label historical evidence SHALL remain display-only and SHALL NOT contribute to the current score or ranking.

#### Scenario: Ranking exposes score version
- **WHEN** the API returns a ranked ETF item generated by the new ranking engine
- **THEN** the item includes a score version and score breakdown showing cross-sectional percentile, dynamic threshold, data reliability, liquidity, and premium or discount components, plus a separate display-only label evidence summary

#### Scenario: Cross-sectional percentile separates close raw scores
- **WHEN** multiple ETFs have similar fixed-window raw metrics
- **THEN** the final ranking uses market-wide and comparable-bucket percentiles to order them rather than returning indistinguishable scores

#### Scenario: Same score field remains compatible
- **WHEN** existing frontend code reads `total_score`
- **THEN** the API still returns `total_score`, with its value derived from the current versioned final score

### Requirement: ETF ranking distinguishes daily and intraday score sources
The system SHALL clearly separate daily reference ranking from fresh intraday ranking for ETFs.

#### Scenario: Fresh intraday ranking exists
- **WHEN** the market is open and a decision-eligible fresh intraday quote is available
- **THEN** the ETF ranking may expose an intraday score source and intraday buy-point context

#### Scenario: Intraday quote is unavailable
- **WHEN** the market is closed or no decision-eligible fresh intraday quote exists
- **THEN** the ETF ranking uses daily reference data and labels the score source as daily or unavailable rather than presenting stale data as realtime

## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Short-Term Scores Are Deterministic And Explainable
The system SHALL rank assets using deterministic short-term metrics and SHALL expose the score breakdown used for each rank. For ETFs, the system SHALL use a versioned final ranking score that combines cross-sectional percentile ranking, dynamic thresholds, data reliability, liquidity quality, and premium or discount penalties; label historical effectiveness SHALL be exposed separately as display-only evidence.

#### Scenario: Ranking uses short-term windows
- **WHEN** the system generates short-term signals
- **THEN** it uses recent windows such as 5, 10, 20, and 60 trading days rather than long-term-only metrics

#### Scenario: ETF ranking uses comparable percentiles
- **WHEN** the system ranks ETFs
- **THEN** it compares ETF metrics using market-wide and comparable-bucket percentiles instead of relying only on fixed absolute score thresholds

#### Scenario: Dynamic thresholds affect ETF score
- **WHEN** an ETF belongs to a high-volatility theme, low-volatility theme, bond-like bucket, cross-border bucket, or other configured bucket
- **THEN** the ranking applies dynamic threshold adjustments appropriate to that bucket and records the adjustment reason

#### Scenario: Historical label evidence is displayed without feedback
- **WHEN** a label combination has sufficient current historical validation evidence for the active rule version
- **THEN** the system records the evidence source, sample count, horizon, and confidence state while leaving the current score, label, rank, allocation, tracking, alert, and notification outputs unchanged

#### Scenario: Risk reduces conclusion strength
- **WHEN** an asset has chase risk, high volatility, large drawdown, stale data, low liquidity where applicable, insufficient market-data history, or abnormal premium or discount
- **THEN** the conclusion is downgraded or the final score is penalized and the risk flags are returned with the ranked item

#### Scenario: Ranking does not require an API key
- **WHEN** no LLM or third-party AI API key is configured
- **THEN** the system still generates deterministic rankings, charts, labels, and rule-based explanations

#### Scenario: AI does not decide ranking
- **WHEN** an AI explanation is generated for a ranked ETF
- **THEN** the AI explanation does not change the deterministic final score, observation label, entry timing label, or ranking order
