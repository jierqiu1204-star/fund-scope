## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: ETF Entry Timing Uses Dynamic Thresholds
The short-term research system SHALL assign ETF entry-timing labels using dynamic thresholds based on volatility, historical percentile, theme context, premium state, and data eligibility.

#### Scenario: Volatile ETF has normal pullback
- **WHEN** an ETF has positive trend, a small pullback within its own normal volatility band, price near or above the relevant moving average, and no high-premium or data-quality risk
- **THEN** the system may label the entry timing as `健康回踩` and exposes the dynamic threshold reason

#### Scenario: Low-volatility ETF has abnormal move
- **WHEN** a low-volatility ETF has a move that is extreme versus its own historical percentile
- **THEN** the system labels the move conservatively even if the absolute percentage move would be normal for a high-volatility ETF

#### Scenario: ETF is high premium
- **WHEN** an ETF has trustworthy high premium or extreme premium
- **THEN** the entry-timing label is downgraded to a premium-risk or avoid-chase state and the detail view explains the premium evidence

### Requirement: ETF Observation Labels Explain Dynamic Threshold Inputs
The short-term research page SHALL explain the dynamic threshold inputs behind ETF observation labels and entry-timing labels.

#### Scenario: User opens ETF detail
- **WHEN** the selected ETF detail uses dynamic thresholds
- **THEN** the UI shows volatility unit, current move versus normal band, historical percentile evidence, premium state where available, and the final label reason in Chinese

#### Scenario: Dynamic context is unavailable
- **WHEN** dynamic threshold inputs are unavailable due to insufficient history or ineligible data
- **THEN** the UI shows `数据不足` or conservative-default wording and does not imply asset-specific confidence

### Requirement: Dynamic Labels Remain Observation-Only
Dynamic ETF labels SHALL remain research labels and SHALL NOT become direct buy or sell instructions.

#### Scenario: Dynamic label is favorable
- **WHEN** an ETF receives `健康回踩` or `趋势延续`
- **THEN** the UI describes it as suitable for continued observation and requires manual judgment rather than saying it should be bought

#### Scenario: Dynamic label is unfavorable
- **WHEN** an ETF receives `冲高别追`, `跌破等待`, `放量转弱`, or premium-risk label
- **THEN** the UI explains the risk and does not present it as an automatic sell instruction unless the user's tracked position separately triggers a holding alert

### Requirement: Dynamic Label Evidence Is Included In Validation
The short-term label validation workflow SHALL record whether a label was generated by fixed or dynamic threshold rules.

#### Scenario: Dynamic labels are replayed
- **WHEN** historical label replay runs after dynamic thresholds are enabled
- **THEN** validation results include rule version, threshold mode, and enough context to compare dynamic labels with historical outcomes
